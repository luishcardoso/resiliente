RESILIENTE is a project conceived by Nenizera and Augusto Haack in 2017 in Vale dos Sinos/RS, Brazil. Two friends who came together to create original and independent music. The band was born from spontaneous exchanges of ideas during long nights of International Superstar Soccer Deluxe with hardcore playing in the background. Resiliente is the communion of genuine feelings, individual experiences, and universal emotions. Their songs mainly reflect on the human condition, with self-criticism and empathy. Their sound stands out for its crossover between Brazilian rhythms, hardcore, and thrash metal. Following the release of their self-titled debut EP in 2025, the band presents a raw and intimate sound, characterized by striking guitar riffs, apocalyptic breakdowns, and visceral vocals. Resiliente is an invitation to reflection and individual awakening; A reminder that even in the most difficult moments of our lives, hope can always be cultivated.

Alone and before the sun...

Our individual experiences are unique, our emotions are universal. Facing the inevitability of suffering in this often wounded world, our heart has a voice, and music is a method through which that voice manifests itself.

In our hands, the blood of tomorrow!

A window to the soul, revealing fragilities and conquests, fears and rebirths, in a constant movement of learning and redemption. An intimate and honest journey, woven by feelings that communicate the highs and lows of everyday life, the resilience in the face of adversity, and the willpower needed to move forward.
